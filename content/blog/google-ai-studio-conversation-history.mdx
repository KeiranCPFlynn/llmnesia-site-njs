Saved Google AI Studio playground conversations can live in your own Google Drive. They are structured JSON files containing your prompts, model replies and settings, often without a `.json` extension. You can download them individually or download the **Google AI Studio** folder as a ZIP.

This gives you a practical backup route as well as another place to look for an old chat. It is separate from the history of the consumer Gemini app.

## AI Studio and Gemini have separate histories

**Gemini Apps**, at gemini.google.com, is Google's consumer assistant. Its conversation history and Google Takeout exports belong to that product.

**Google AI Studio**, at aistudio.google.com, is a playground and development environment for Gemini models. Saved playground prompts and chats can be stored as files in Google Drive. Do not assume a Gemini Takeout export contains those AI Studio files.

AI Studio also offers app building, media generation and live sessions. Their storage and export behaviour can differ. The Drive instructions below concern saved playground prompt and chat files.

## Find a saved conversation

1. Sign in to AI Studio with the Google account you used for the chat.
2. Open **History** or **Library** in the navigation.
3. Find the saved conversation by its title and open it.

If it is missing there, check the **Google AI Studio** folder in Google Drive using the same account. Account switching and a chat that never finished saving are different problems, so check both before assuming data was deleted.

A browser-history URL may also reopen a saved chat. A link to the blank new-chat page cannot recover an unsaved conversation.

## Download AI Studio chats from Google Drive

You do not need an extension or a Google Drive integration to make a local backup:

1. Open Google Drive and find the **Google AI Studio** folder.
2. To download one chat, right-click its file and choose **Download**.
3. To download several chats, select their files and choose **Download**. You can also download the folder. Drive packages folder downloads as a ZIP.
4. Keep the download somewhere you back up regularly. Extract a ZIP if you want to inspect the individual files.

The files may have conversation titles as their names and no filename extension. That does not make them empty or unreadable. Their contents are JSON, which a text editor can open.

The JSON includes provider settings and message metadata as well as conversation text. A transcript reader needs to distinguish prompts and final answers from thinking, tool data and unsent inputs. Removing arbitrary lines is not a reliable way to convert it.

Images, audio and video may appear as references to separate Drive files. Downloading the chat JSON alone does not guarantee a complete offline copy of its media.

**Keep the originals in Drive.** Downloading is sufficient for a backup. Google attaches special metadata to AI Studio files; downloading and re-uploading them as ordinary files can lose that metadata, so AI Studio may no longer recognise them as saved chats. Do not delete the original files while experimenting with a copy.

## Search AI Studio chats with LLMnesia

AI Studio uses **manual capture** in LLMnesia:

1. Open the conversation in AI Studio and wait for the answer to finish.
2. Click the LLMnesia pill, whose hint says **Click to save**. The compact pill expands when you hover over it.
3. Open LLMnesia search and search for words from the conversation.
4. After adding more messages, click the pill again to refresh the saved copy.

**Browsing History or Library does not automatically import past chats.** Each conversation must be opened and saved, unless you use the file-import option described below. Automatic indexing on other supported platforms should not be confused with AI Studio's manual save flow.

### Import downloaded Drive files

If your installed version shows **Import Google AI Studio chats** in LLMnesia's Settings, you can choose individual downloaded chat files or a folder ZIP. Extensionless files are accepted, so you do not need to rename them first.

Files are parsed on your device without connecting LLMnesia to Google Drive. The importer saves conversation text and system instructions, preserves available model names and dates, and leaves out thinking, tool activity and media references. If Vault is enabled, imported chats sync under your existing settings.

You can stop between chats and choose the same files again to resume. Re-importing the same download updates its imported copy rather than creating another imported copy. Downloads generally omit their original Drive chat IDs, so these copies do not link back to AI Studio and may be separate from conversations you previously saved with the browser pill.

If the import option is absent, your installed version requires the manual save workflow above. Keep your Drive downloads as a backup; LLMnesia's **Import backup** option expects a LLMnesia backup, not an AI Studio JSON file.

## What to keep when backing up prompt work

Keep the saved conversation file, useful system instructions, and any separate media you need. Model names and generation settings help explain how an answer was produced, but they are different from the conversation itself.

Give important chats descriptive names, wait for Drive saving to complete, and keep a local backup of work you need to retain. Neither browser history nor a searchable index can recreate a conversation that was never saved or captured.
