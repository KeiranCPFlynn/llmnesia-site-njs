You can use ChatGPT without saving conversations in two ways: start a Temporary Chat, which leaves no record at all, or turn off model training, which keeps your history for you while stopping OpenAI from learning from it. These solve different problems, and people often pick the wrong one. This post explains how each works, the trade-offs, and how to keep a private record when you do want to find answers later.

## Two different goals, two different settings

Before changing anything, decide what you actually want.

| Your goal | Use | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Leave no saved record of this chat | Temporary Chat | Not saved, not trained on, gone after |
| Keep history for yourself but not feed training | Turn off model training | Saved for you, excluded from training |
| Remove chats you already had | Delete conversations | Cleared from your account |

Mixing these up is the most common mistake. Temporary Chat is for "I do not want this saved." The training toggle is for "save it for me, just do not learn from it."

## Option 1: Temporary Chat (leave no record)

Temporary Chat is the simplest way to use ChatGPT without saving a conversation. According to OpenAI, a Temporary Chat does not appear in your history, does not create or use memories, and is not used to train models.

To use it, start a Temporary Chat and have your conversation as normal. When you finish, it is not added to your history.

The trade-off is total: because nothing is saved, you cannot return to it later. Temporary Chat is ideal for a sensitive one-off question and unsuitable for anything you might need to reference again.

## Option 2: turn off model training (keep history, stop training)

If you want to keep your conversations for your own retrieval but not have them used to improve OpenAI's models:

1. Open **Settings**, then **Data Controls**.
2. Turn off **Improve the model for everyone**.

Your history stays on and your conversations remain available to you, but new conversations are not used for training. This is the right choice for regular work where you want a record but not the training. See [does ChatGPT train on your conversations?](/blog/does-chatgpt-train-on-your-conversations) for the full detail.

## What "without saving" does and does not protect

Be realistic about the boundary of these settings:

- **Temporary Chat** keeps a conversation out of your history and out of training. It does not guarantee no copy exists anywhere; providers can retain content briefly for safety and abuse-prevention.
- Neither option affects **device or network monitoring**. On a work computer or corporate network, those layers still see your activity. See [can my employer see my ChatGPT conversations?](/blog/can-my-employer-see-my-chatgpt-conversations).
- Using ChatGPT "without saving" is about your account record and training, not about being invisible at every layer.

## The retrieval trade-off, and how to solve it

The catch with not saving history is obvious in hindsight: you lose the ability to find anything later. For a one-off question that is fine. For ongoing work it is a problem, because the value of AI conversations compounds when you can reuse past answers.

The better setup for most people who care about privacy but still need their history:

1. Keep history on.
2. Turn off model training so OpenAI does not learn from your chats.
3. Use a local-first tool so your searchable record lives on your device, not just on the platform.

LLMnesia is a free, local-first Chrome extension that searches your AI chat history across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and 10+ platforms. It indexes your conversations on your own device as you browse them, so you keep a full-text searchable archive locally while staying opted out of training. The index is never uploaded to LLMnesia's servers. See [local-first AI tools and privacy](/blog/local-first-ai-tools-privacy).

If instead you want to remove conversations you already have, see [how to delete all ChatGPT conversations](/blog/how-to-delete-all-chatgpt-conversations).

## Quick recommendation

- For a single sensitive question: use **Temporary Chat**.
- For ongoing work where you want privacy but also a record: keep history on, **turn off training**, and index locally.
- Remember neither setting hides activity from a work device or network.
