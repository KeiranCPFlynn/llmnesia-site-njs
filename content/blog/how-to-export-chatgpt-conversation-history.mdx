Use ChatGPT's account export when you want a backup of your accessible history. Use a single-chat exporter when you need one readable conversation. After downloading, check the contents before depending on the archive or feeding it into another assistant.

## Request the account export

1. Sign in to the ChatGPT account you want to export.
2. Open your profile menu, then **Settings**.
3. Open **Data controls**.
4. Under **Export data**, select **Export**, then **Confirm export**.
5. Download the archive from the notification while signed in to the same account.

OpenAI says preparation can take up to seven days and the link expires 24 hours after delivery. Self-service export is unavailable in Business, Enterprise and Healthcare workspaces; eligible Edu workspaces have additional conditions. Consult the [official instructions](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/7260999-exporting-your-chatgpt-history-and-data) for your account, or use the linked Privacy Portal.

The ZIP contains chat history and other account data. It cannot restore previously deleted chats. Download and check it before removing a personal workspace or making a move that could remove access.

## Check what you received

Keep the original ZIP and inspect an extracted copy. File layouts can change. Look for the conversation files rather than assuming every export has an identical structure.

Open several known conversations, including a long one, and check speaker labels, first and last messages, dates and any content you need to preserve. Treat images, attachments, tool results, project information and saved memories as separate things to verify. Do not infer their completeness from the presence of the main transcript.

If the archive contains a readable HTML history, open it in a browser and search with Cmd+F or Ctrl+F. JSON conversation files can be searched in a text editor or processed by an importer. Keep all conversation parts together if your export is split across files.

## Save one chat as a readable file

[ChatGPT Exporter](https://www.chatgptexporter.com/en) offers PDF, Markdown, TXT, CSV, JSON and image output from selected conversations. Its pricing page lists three free PDFs daily. Its FAQ says PDF generation temporarily processes data on its servers, while other formats run in your browser.

[AI Toolbox](https://www.ai-toolbox.co/) is another option when you also want search and organisation across several AI websites. Single-chat TXT export is free; additional formats require Premium.

Choose a format for the destination: a readable document for sharing, plain text for broad compatibility, or structured data for processing. Verify the exported result against the conversation before relying on code, tables or complex responses. For the exact product distinctions, see [LLMnesia vs ChatGPT Exporter](/compare/llmnesia-vs-chatgpt-exporter).

## Search older history with LLMnesia

LLMnesia supports importing older ChatGPT history, including its export-file route. You do not need to reopen every old chat manually to make a downloaded export useful. Follow the import option shown in LLMnesia, keep the original archive and check that a known conversation appears in search afterward.

Supported new conversations can then be captured as you work. Local imports and search are free and require no account. Optional [Vault](/vault) adds paid end-to-end encrypted backup and sync; the [free MCP connection](/mcp) lets compatible desktop assistants consult captured history.

## Use the export when switching assistants

An exported conversation can be useful context. Select the threads relevant to your next task, convert them into a format the receiving assistant supports and add a short note stating the current goal and unresolved questions.

Uploading transcripts is different from restoring them in another platform's native history. It also places the supplied content under the receiving service's privacy terms.
