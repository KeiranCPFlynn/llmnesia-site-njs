**On a computer, open Gemini's menu and choose Search. On a phone, open the menu and use the search field.** Sign into the Google account that owns the chats. Use the recent list to browse, or search for a topic you remember.

## Find and search Gemini history on a computer

1. Open [gemini.google.com](https://gemini.google.com/) and check the signed-in account.
2. Click **Menu** if the side panel is hidden. Your pinned and recent chats appear there.
3. Choose **Search**, type a topic or distinctive term, and press Enter.
4. Open a matching chat and check that it contains the answer you wanted.

These steps follow [Google's computer guide](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13666746?hl=en). It does not define matching as title-only. Try a shorter term if a longer phrase gives no useful match.

## Find Gemini history on Android, iPhone or iPad

Open the Gemini app or [gemini.google.com](https://gemini.google.com/) in your mobile browser. Tap **Menu** to see **Recent** and the search field, then enter a topic. Google's [Android instructions](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13666746?hl=en&co=GENIE.Platform%3DAndroid) and [iPhone instructions](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13666746?hl=en&co=GENIE.Platform%3DiOS) cover both routes.

The steps above use Gemini itself. LLMnesia's Chrome or Edge extension runs on a computer; installing it there does not add a search control to the Gemini mobile app.

## If your Gemini history is missing

Check the account before changing anything. For personal accounts, saved history depends on **Keep Activity**; work and school settings may be controlled by an administrator. A temporary or deleted chat may not appear. Turning activity on now does not recreate a conversation that was never saved.

Use the [missing-chat checklist](/blog/how-to-find-old-gemini-conversations) to separate an account or loading problem from confirmed deletion. For how long activity is kept, see the [retention settings guide](/blog/gemini-conversation-history-limits).

## Find a passage inside an open chat

Once you have the right conversation, use Ctrl+F on Windows or Cmd+F on Mac. This searches the text currently rendered in that page. Older turns may need to load before the browser can find them.

Finding text in one open chat is different from searching an archive across several assistants.

## Search a local copy with LLMnesia

LLMnesia can capture Gemini conversations as you browse and import accessible older Gemini chats through its supported history importer. Installing it alone does not make all previous history searchable.

1. [Install LLMnesia](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/llmnesia-search-all-your/leekfgbdojiaabifbjbbgiiclannjdkf) in the browser where you use Gemini.
2. Sign into Gemini and use LLMnesia's history-import controls, or open a conversation to capture it.
3. Check the import status. A partial or failed import is not a complete archive.
4. Open LLMnesia search and enter a distinctive word from a conversation that was saved.
5. Open the matching result to check the answer.

Keyword search becomes available after local keyword indexing. Semantic processing can continue separately. Neither can retrieve a conversation the importer could not access or a deleted chat that was never saved.

Local search does not require a LLMnesia account. Accessing your Gemini history still requires the appropriate Google account. Optional paid Vault is separate from local capture and search.

## Check activity or an export when needed

Google's [activity controls](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13278892?hl=en) help explain whether history was saved or removed. My Activity is not an independent recovery backup after deletion.

For an offline export, follow Google's [Gemini download instructions](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16920332?hl=en). Chat activity is selected under **My Activity**, then **Gemini Apps**; the separate **Gemini** selection concerns Gems. Inspect the archive before relying on it. A new export cannot recreate already-deleted data.

## Choose the right retrieval method

| What you need | Where to start | What it covers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Find a Gemini chat | Gemini's menu and native search | Chats still available in that Google account |
| Find a passage in one chat | Browser find on a computer | Text currently loaded in the page |
| Search Gemini alongside other assistants | LLMnesia on a computer | Conversations successfully saved and locally indexed |
| Read a chat after the original disappears | An existing export or saved copy | Only the content that copy actually contains |

For example, a phrase from a saved project discussion can find the same work across Gemini, Claude and ChatGPT in LLMnesia. First capture or import the relevant conversations, wait for keyword indexing, and open a result to verify the saved text. Native Gemini search remains the starting point for a chat that exists only in your Google account.

If you are trying to recover something deliberately deleted, see [what is and is not recoverable](/blog/recover-deleted-gemini-conversation).
