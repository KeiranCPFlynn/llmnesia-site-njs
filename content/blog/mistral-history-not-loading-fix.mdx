If your Mistral history is not loading, the issue is typically caused by a corrupted browser cache, an active ad blocker interfering with Mistral's API endpoints, or a temporary server-side synchronization lag on Mistral's infrastructure. Like most AI platforms, Mistral Vibe (formerly known as Le Chat) only indexes conversation titles natively in its sidebar rather than full-text message content, making even a temporary loading error a major disruption for retrieving past work. Fortunately, most sidebar loading issues can be resolved in under five minutes using local browser troubleshooting or alternative access workarounds.

## Why is Your Mistral Chat History Missing?
When the left sidebar on `chat.mistral.ai` remains empty or displays a spinning loading wheel, your past conversations have not necessarily been deleted. Because Mistral Vibe retrieves your chat logs from their servers dynamically every time you load the page, any breakdown in that data pathway results in a blank sidebar.

The most common causes of this issue include:
1. **Server-side downtime:** Mistral's servers are experiencing high traffic, causing delayed API responses.
2. **Local cache corruption:** Old browser session data is conflicting with recent updates to the Mistral interface.
3. **Ad blockers and security extensions:** Aggressive browser extensions are blocking the WebSocket connections or background API requests that populate your sidebar.
4. **The "empty sidebar" archive glitch:** A known interface bug where archiving all active chats hides the navigation elements needed to access them.

Before attempting complex fixes, a simple hard refresh (pressing `Ctrl + F5` on Windows or `Cmd + Shift + R` on Mac) is always the best first diagnostic step.

## Clear Your Browser Cache and Site Data for Mistral
A corrupted browser cache is the leading cause of persistent loading states. If Mistral deployed a system update, your browser might still attempt to use cached assets from an older session, preventing the sidebar from rendering correctly.

To clear site data specifically for Mistral in Google Chrome or any Chromium-based browser:
1. Navigate to the Mistral Vibe interface.
2. Click the padlock icon located on the left side of your browser's address bar.
3. Select **Site settings** from the dropdown menu.
4. Under the **Usage** section, click the **Clear data** button.
5. Reload the page and log back into your Mistral account.

This action will clear your local storage and cookies for the Mistral domain without affecting other sites or deleting your actual chat history. Once you log back in, your sidebar should rebuild itself from Mistral's servers.

## Disable Conflicting Browser Extensions
Security extensions, ad blockers, and tracking protection tools are highly prone to blocking the background network requests that Mistral Vibe uses to sync your history. If your extensions flag Mistral's data sync scripts as trackers, your sidebar will remain completely empty.

To determine if an extension is causing the conflict:
1. Open a new Incognito or Private Browsing window (which typically disables most extensions by default).
2. Go to the Mistral Vibe login page and sign in.
3. If your chat history loads normally in Incognito mode, an active browser extension is the culprit.

To resolve this, return to your standard browser window and temporarily disable your ad blockers or whitelist the `mistral.ai` domain. Pay close attention to extensions that block scripts, manage cookies, or modify network headers.

## Verify if Mistral Vibe is Experiencing a Server Outage
Mistral frequently updates its models and system infrastructure, which can occasionally trigger temporary database synchronization lag or outright server downtime. During these high-load events, the primary chat interface may load, but the API calls responsible for fetching your history will fail.

You can verify the current operational state of Mistral's platform through these official and community resources:
* **Official Status Page:** Check the system health monitors on the official status page to see if there are active incidents affecting the chat API.
* **Community Boards:** Look at community discussions on Reddit or developer forums to see if other users are reporting similar loading failures.

If a server outage is confirmed, your chat history is safe but temporarily unreachable. You will need to wait for Mistral's technical team to resolve the issue on their backend.

## Force the Archived Chats Link to Reappear
Mistral Vibe features an archiving system that allows you to clean up your main sidebar. However, a unique interface bug occurs if you archive all your active chats or move every conversation into specific projects: the link to access archived chats can completely disappear from the user interface.

If you suspect your history is missing because it was archived but you can no longer find the archive folder, follow this simple workaround:
1. Click the **New Chat** button in the top left corner of the Mistral interface.
2. Type a short test prompt and submit it to the AI.
3. Once the new chat is active and appears in your sidebar, hover your cursor over the sidebar options or click the three-dot menu icon.
4. The archived chats option should instantly reappear, allowing you to access and restore your older conversations.

If your sidebar remains empty even after trying these methods, you can attempt to [find old Mistral conversations](/blog/how-to-find-old-mistral-conversations) using your browser's history or inspect your browser's network console for active connection blocks.
