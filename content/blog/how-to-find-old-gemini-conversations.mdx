An old Gemini chat may be in another account, difficult to find, or no longer retained. Check your account and Gemini's **Search** before assuming it is deleted. There is no guarantee that every previous conversation still exists.

## 1. Confirm the account and search the saved chats

Open Gemini's side panel and use **Search** with a distinctive topic or phrase. If you have several Google accounts, check the one you used for the conversation. Look through recent chats too, especially if the title differs from the wording you remember.

Google documents these controls in its [chat-management help](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13666746?hl=en). For a passage inside a chat you have already found, use browser find. The [Gemini search guide](/blog/search-gemini-conversation-history) explains the difference.

## 2. Check whether the history was retained

Review **Keep Activity** and the applicable retention settings. Account policies, auto-deletion, deliberate deletion and temporary chats can affect availability. Work or school accounts may be managed by an administrator.

Do not turn settings on expecting past data to reappear. Read Google's [activity-management guidance](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13278892?hl=en) before changing privacy settings.

## 3. Check browser history for the original URL

Search your browser history for `gemini.google.com/app` and the approximate date. A matching URL can help you return to a chat that still exists and is accessible.

A history entry contains a location, not a saved transcript. Reopening it does not undo deletion. If the entire Gemini sidebar fails to load, use the [history-loading troubleshooting guide](/blog/gemini-history-not-loading-fix).

## 4. Search or import a local copy

If LLMnesia already saved the conversation, search its local index for a distinctive term. You can open a saved copy in its Viewer rather than relying solely on the original provider page.

If you have only just installed LLMnesia, accessible older Gemini chats can be imported through the supported history importer:

1. [Install LLMnesia](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/llmnesia-search-all-your/leekfgbdojiaabifbjbbgiiclannjdkf) and sign into the right Gemini account.
2. Start the Gemini history import from LLMnesia's import controls.
3. Check which conversations were actually saved and whether the run is partial or needs retry.
4. Search for a word from a successfully indexed conversation.
5. Open the result and verify the content.

The importer cannot restore an already-deleted chat or guarantee access to all historical conversations. Keyword indexing must finish before that saved content is searchable; semantic processing is separate.

## 5. Look for a backup you already had

Check an existing export, copied transcript or saved local record. Inspect it for the specific conversation, not just the title or an activity entry.

Deleting a chat from Gemini also deletes the related Gemini Apps Activity, according to Google. My Activity is therefore not a second undeletable copy. See the [deleted-chat guide](/blog/recover-deleted-gemini-conversation) for realistic next steps.

For future exports, follow [Google's current download instructions](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16920332?hl=en). Keep a verified offline copy of important work before deleting the original.
