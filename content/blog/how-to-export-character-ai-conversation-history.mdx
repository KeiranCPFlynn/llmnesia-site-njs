**Request your Character.AI account export from Settings, and save important chats separately while you wait.** Character.AI's current help article says processing can take 3 to 4 weeks and the emailed download link lasts 7 days. Its instructions do not specify the file format or guarantee which conversations will be included.

## Current status, checked October 22, 2026

Character.AI announced a Chapter 11 sale process on October 12. It says the service remains available while it seeks a buyer, and may close if no suitable bid allows continued operation. This is not a confirmed shutdown date. See [Character.AI's announcement](https://blog.character.ai/an-update-on-character-ai/). Keep copies of conversations that matter to you, and check that you can read them independently of the site.

## Request the export on the web

1. Sign in to the account whose data you want to keep and open your profile.
2. Open Profile Settings, then Account.
3. Open Manage Account & Data and choose Export Data.
4. Watch the email address associated with that account for the download link.
5. Download the file within 7 days of the email, retain the original, and check its contents.

## Request the export in the mobile app

Open Profile, tap Settings, then Legal & Privacy, then Export Data. Character.AI says a success banner confirms the request. Its [current instructions](https://support.character.ai/hc/en-us/articles/56967715028891-How-do-I-download-my-data) differ from the wording in its older help article, which calls this Export my data.

Allow 3 to 4 weeks. That is the published processing estimate, not a guaranteed delivery date. If an export cannot be opened, follow any instructions in the accompanying email and ask Character.AI support for help. Do not assume another app accepts the archive just because it accepts some Character.AI files.

## Save a readable copy today

Open a chat you care about and load the earlier messages you want to preserve. Copy the text into a local document. You can also try Print, then Save as PDF, but inspect the resulting pages: a browser can only print content that has loaded, and long chats may be incomplete.

Record the character's name and the chat's date. If you created the character, separately keep your own description and definition. A transcript does not preserve a model or guarantee identical behaviour in another app.

## Read and search chats with LLMnesia

LLMnesia supports Character.AI live capture and history import from a signed-in desktop browser. Version 0.4.19 includes discovery of older sessions with the same character and archived chats. Source availability, authentication and request failures can still leave gaps. Check the saved count and inspect important chats rather than assuming everything was captured.

Saved copies can be read in LLMnesia and copied as transcripts. LLMnesia saves and searches captured text locally. Optional Vault sync uploads encrypted copies when you enable it. Neither capture nor import can recover messages the source no longer provides. A search index is not a substitute for a separate backup: removing an extension or clearing its storage can remove local copies.


## Where can an export be imported?

Compatibility depends on the exact format. CAI Tools character cards describe a character; they are not the same as chat history. A destination that accepts Tavern chat files may still reject an official account archive.

LLMnesia's saved-file importer and Markdown download are in development and are not included in the Chrome Web Store version checked on October 22 (0.4.19). The planned account-message reader is experimental, so this guide does not promise official-export compatibility. Keep your original download and verify any imported copy before relying on it.
