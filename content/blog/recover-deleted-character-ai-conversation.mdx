**An existing saved copy is the most useful recovery route.** LLMnesia can show text it captured before a chat became unavailable. It cannot undelete a conversation from Character.AI or reconstruct messages it never saved.

## Current status, checked October 22, 2026

Character.AI announced a Chapter 11 sale process on October 12. It says the service remains available while it seeks a buyer, and may close if no suitable bid allows continued operation. This is not a confirmed shutdown date. See [Character.AI's announcement](https://blog.character.ai/an-update-on-character-ai/). Keep copies of conversations that matter to you, and check that you can read them independently of the site.

## Establish what is missing

Confirm the account and character, then check other sessions with that character. A blank or incomplete history view can be a loading issue. Try [history loading checks](/blog/character-ai-history-not-loading-fix) before concluding that the chat was deleted.

## Preserve anything still visible

If the conversation is open in a tab and its text remains visible, copy it into a local document before refreshing. A browser back button or reopened tab might preserve a view, but it may also reload from the service. Do not rely on browser cache as a recovery mechanism. Browser history normally saves addresses, not complete transcripts.

## Look for backups made before the loss

Search your existing LLMnesia saved copies by a distinctive phrase. Open the saved transcript and check its start and end. Also check earlier account exports, documents, PDFs or screenshots that you deliberately saved. An export requested after deletion is not a guaranteed way to retrieve the deleted text.

If you install LLMnesia now, history import can save chats still accessible from Character.AI. Version 0.4.23 adds an optional Recover missing chats action, which tries every chat not yet saved on this device. After you confirm, it can unarchive archived chats that cannot be read, and they stay unarchived even if saving fails. This can affect hundreds of chats, and Character.AI may still refuse some. It cannot recover a deleted chat that the provider no longer returns. Contact Character.AI support if you need confirmation of what remains available; no recovery is promised here.

LLMnesia saves and searches captured text locally. Optional Vault sync uploads encrypted copies when you enable it. Neither capture nor import can recover messages the source no longer provides. A search index is not a substitute for a separate backup: removing an extension or clearing its storage can remove local copies.

## Protect the next story

Save important passages in a separate file, record the character and session date, and request the official account export while access remains available. Its published processing estimate is 3 to 4 weeks and the emailed download link lasts 7 days. Open and inspect the downloaded files; the official instructions do not guarantee their complete contents or compatibility with another app.
