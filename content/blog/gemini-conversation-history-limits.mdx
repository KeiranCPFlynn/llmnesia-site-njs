**For personal accounts, Gemini Apps activity is set to auto-delete after 18 months by default.** You can choose 3 or 36 months instead, or switch off auto-deletion. Check your account: temporary chats, activity-off conversations and work or school accounts follow different rules.

## Compare Gemini history settings

| Setting or account | What to check | What it does not guarantee |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Keep Activity on, default auto-delete | Activity older than 18 months is automatically deleted | A separate backup of deleted chats |
| Custom auto-delete | Choose 3 or 36 months | Recovery of activity already removed |
| Don't auto-delete activity | Automatic age-based deletion is off | Permanent access after manual deletion or loss of account access |
| Keep Activity off or a temporary chat | Google describes short-term retention of up to 72 hours | A recoverable history archive |
| Work or school account | Ask your Workspace administrator about the applicable policy | The same settings or retention as a personal account |

Google's [activity controls](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13278892?hl=en) and [Privacy Hub](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13594961?hl=en) explain these differences. Server-side retention is not the same as a conversation being available to reopen.

## Check or change your auto-delete period

On a computer, open Gemini, choose **Settings & help**, then **Activity**. You can also open [Gemini Apps Activity](https://myactivity.google.com/product/gemini) directly. Check the signed-in account, open the auto-delete option near the top, choose a period or **Don't auto-delete activity**, then follow Google's confirmation steps.

Review the choice before confirming it. Selecting a shorter period can remove older activity. Changing retention later does not bring back conversations already deleted. For managed accounts, these controls may not be available.

## A saved history list is not a backup

Deleting a chat or its related activity can remove it from your accessible history. Changing retention settings later cannot reconstruct a deleted conversation.

Before removing important work, verify a copy you control. A copied URL, browser-history entry or activity title is not the same as the full conversation.

## Context windows describe active processing

A context window limits how much information a model can use at once. Practical limits vary by model and product surface, so API token limits should not be presented as a guarantee for the consumer Gemini app.

There is no universal conversation-count guarantee in the settings above. This is separate from how many chats your account retains. If you need to find something in a saved chat, start with [Gemini's native Search](/blog/search-gemini-conversation-history), not a context-window estimate. Google's chat-search help does not establish a title-only matching restriction.

## Keep an inspectable offline export

Use [Google's Gemini export guide](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16920332?hl=en). Select Gemini chat activity through **My Activity**, then **Gemini Apps**. The separate **Gemini** option is for Gems.

Download and inspect the archive for the conversations and text you need. Export contents depend on the data available to the account and the archive's timing. An export request is not confirmation that every desired conversation is safely backed up.

## Build a searchable local copy

LLMnesia captures supported Gemini conversations while you browse and can import accessible older history. Its keyword index and searches stay on your device. Optional paid Vault backup and sync are separate.

1. [Install LLMnesia](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/llmnesia-search-all-your/leekfgbdojiaabifbjbbgiiclannjdkf) and capture or import a conversation.
2. Wait for that conversation to be saved and keyword-indexed.
3. Search a distinctive term and open the result.
4. Check that the saved copy contains the content you care about.

A partial import only covers what was successfully saved. Semantic processing can continue after keyword search is available. Neither process retrieves inaccessible history or repairs an original deleted before capture.

If chats appear missing, follow the [older-conversation checks](/blog/how-to-find-old-gemini-conversations). If deletion is confirmed, use the [recovery guide](/blog/recover-deleted-gemini-conversation) without assuming Google Activity holds a separate copy.
