**Stored history, a character's memory, and your own backup are different things.** A conversation can remain in history even when the model stops recalling its earlier details. A saved local copy is useful when you need the actual words again.

## Current status, checked October 22, 2026

Character.AI announced a Chapter 11 sale process on October 12. It says the service remains available while it seeks a buyer, and may close if no suitable bid allows continued operation. This is not a confirmed shutdown date. See [Character.AI's announcement](https://blog.character.ai/an-update-on-character-ai/). Keep copies of conversations that matter to you, and check that you can read them independently of the site.

## History and retention

Character.AI provides history controls and an account export request. Do not interpret an account being active as a promise of permanent storage. Older or archived sessions may be harder to discover, and access depends on the service and account remaining available. Check the current privacy policy for retention terms rather than relying on an assumed unlimited lifetime.

## Memory is not a transcript archive

A model has limits on the information it can use in a response. Character.AI also offers memory features, but neither a memory feature nor a character's apparent recollection guarantees access to every earlier message. Keep important facts in your own notes and save the conversation if its exact wording matters.

## Export exists, but needs checking

Character.AI has a native account data export. On web, use Profile Settings, Account, Manage Account & Data, Export Data. On mobile, use Settings, Legal & Privacy, Export Data. Processing may take 3 to 4 weeks, and the emailed link is valid for 7 days. Its published instructions do not specify the archive format or guarantee all chats are included.

## Make saved chats easier to find

LLMnesia adds local full-text search across captured conversations. Live capture and history import can save accessible chats; version 0.4.19 includes older sessions and archived-chat discovery. This remains subject to source availability and import errors, so verify the chats you care about.

LLMnesia saves and searches captured text locally. Optional Vault sync uploads encrypted copies when you enable it. Neither capture nor import can recover messages the source no longer provides. A search index is not a substitute for a separate backup: removing an extension or clearing its storage can remove local copies.

## A practical preservation routine

Request the official export, copy irreplaceable passages into a separate file, and check the first and last messages of each important saved chat. Keep character names and dates with your copies. A transcript can preserve your writing without recreating the original character or model elsewhere.
