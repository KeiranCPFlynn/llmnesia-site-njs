AI chats are not deleted automatically on most major platforms. Your conversations stay in your history until you delete them yourself or close your account, with the exact deletion timing and whether anything is recoverable afterwards set by each provider's privacy policy. The main exception is temporary or incognito-style chat modes, which are deliberately designed not to be saved to your history at all.

That single distinction, between conversations the platform keeps and modes it is built to forget, clears up most of the confusion. This guide walks through what actually happens to your AI conversations: what is retained, what deletion really does, and how to keep a copy you control.

## The default: chats are kept, not auto-deleted

On ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and the other mainstream assistants, the normal behaviour is that every conversation is saved to your account and shown in a sidebar. The platforms do not run a timer that quietly erases old chats. A conversation from six months ago is, by default, still there.

What this means in practice:

- **Your history accumulates** until you act on it. It does not expire on its own.
- **It is account-bound.** Sign in on another device and the same history appears, because it lives on the provider's servers, not your machine.
- **You are the one who triggers deletion,** by removing a conversation, clearing history, or deleting the account.

There is no universal retention clock across providers, which is why "do AI chats get deleted" has no single yes-or-no answer. For a platform-by-platform view of how long history sticks around, see [how long do AI platforms keep history](/blog/how-long-do-ai-platforms-keep-history).

## What deletion actually does

When you delete a conversation, two things happen on different timelines.

**Immediately:** the conversation disappears from your visible history. From your point of view it is gone.

**Behind the scenes:** the provider may keep a backend copy for a limited window before permanent removal. Most major providers describe a delay between a user deletion and full removal from their systems, partly for operational reasons like backups and partly for safety and legal obligations. Content that was already incorporated into model training, or that sits in system backups, can persist beyond the visible deletion.

The important takeaway is that "deleted from your sidebar" and "permanently erased everywhere" are not the same moment. The first is instant; the second is governed by the provider's policy. The provider-specific detail for OpenAI is covered in [does ChatGPT keep deleted conversations](/blog/does-chatgpt-keep-deleted-conversations).

## Temporary and incognito modes are the real exception

The one case where AI chats are deliberately not saved is temporary chat modes. Several platforms offer a mode that does not write the conversation to your history. These are built for one-off or sensitive questions you do not want stored.

A few things to understand about them:

- They are designed not to appear in your history afterwards, so do not expect to find them later.
- Providers may still retain such conversations briefly for abuse monitoring before deleting them, per their policies.
- If you want a record of a temporary chat, you must save it yourself at the time, because it will not be retrievable from history.

If you used a temporary mode and now cannot find the conversation, it is not a bug. It worked as intended.

## Can deleted conversations be recovered?

Generally, no, not from the interface. Once you delete a conversation on the major platforms, there is no built-in "restore" or "trash" you can fish it out of. The platforms treat user-initiated deletion as final from your side.

| Scenario | Recoverable by you? |
|---|---|
| Conversation still in your history | Yes, just open it |
| Conversation you deleted | Usually not, no restore option |
| Temporary or incognito chat | No, it was never saved |
| Conversation lost to an account issue | Sometimes, via provider support |
| Sidebar simply not loading | Yes, it is a display issue, not deletion |

If a chat is missing but you did not delete it, check whether the sidebar is failing to load rather than assuming the conversation is gone. That is a different problem with a different fix. If you genuinely deleted something on ChatGPT, see [recover deleted ChatGPT conversation](/blog/recover-deleted-chatgpt-conversation) for the realistic options.

## The reliable protection: keep your own copy

Because deletion is generally irreversible and retention rules are outside your control, the only dependable way to never lose an important AI conversation is to not rely on the platform's history as your single copy.

Two habits cover it:

1. **Export periodically.** Most platforms offer a data export in settings that gives you a downloadable archive. Do this on a schedule for anything you would hate to lose.
2. **Index locally as you go.** A local-first tool keeps a searchable copy on your device automatically, so the record exists independently of the platform.

[LLMnesia](https://llmnesia.com/) is a free, local-first Chrome extension that handles the second habit. As you browse ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, and other supported platforms, it indexes the conversations on your own device and makes them full-text searchable.

- **Local-first:** the index lives on your machine, not uploaded to a server.
- **Independent of the platform:** because the searchable copy is yours, it survives a deletion, an account problem, or a platform change.
- **Cross-platform:** one search covers every AI tool you use.

[Install LLMnesia from the Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/llmnesia/leekfgbdojiaabifbjbbgiiclannjdkf) so that the conversations you care about are captured before any deletion can remove them.

## In summary

AI chats do not get deleted automatically on the major platforms; they stay in your history until you delete them, and the timing and finality of that deletion follow each provider's policy. Temporary chat modes are the deliberate exception, built not to be saved. Deletion is generally irreversible from your side, so the protection that matters is keeping your own copy, through periodic exports and a local-first index that holds a searchable record on your device.
