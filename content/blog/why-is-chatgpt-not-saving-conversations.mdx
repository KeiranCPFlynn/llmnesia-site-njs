If ChatGPT is not saving your conversations, it is almost always a setting or account issue rather than data loss. The usual culprits are Temporary Chat (which is designed not to save), chat history being turned off, being signed into a different account than you expect, or a loading glitch hiding new chats. Work through the causes below in order, from most to least common, and you will usually find that the conversations are recoverable or that a simple setting change fixes future ones.

## The common causes, in order

| Cause | How to tell | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Temporary Chat | You started the chat in Temporary mode | Start a normal chat; Temporary never saves |
| History turned off | Data Controls shows history disabled | Turn history back on |
| Wrong account | You have multiple ChatGPT logins | Sign in with the right account |
| Loading or sync glitch | History is on but chats do not appear | Hard refresh, clear cache, check status |

The first two account for most "ChatGPT is not saving my chats" reports, and both are settings, not faults.

## Cause 1: you are in a Temporary Chat

Temporary Chat is the single most common reason. By design, a Temporary Chat does not appear in your history, does not use or update memory, and is not used for training. If your new conversations keep vanishing, check whether you started them in Temporary mode. Switch to a normal chat and it will save. If you actually want non-saving behaviour on purpose, see [how to use ChatGPT without saving history](/blog/how-to-use-chatgpt-without-saving-history).

## Cause 2: chat history is turned off

ChatGPT has a setting that controls whether conversations are saved to your sidebar. If it is off, new chats are not stored. To check:

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Go to **Data Controls**.
3. If chat history is off, turn it back on.

Important: conversations you had while history was off were never saved and cannot be recovered. Turning it on only affects future chats.

## Cause 3: you are on a different account

If you have more than one ChatGPT login (personal, Google, work), new chats save to whichever account you are currently in. If you are looking at a different account's sidebar, your chats will appear to be missing. Confirm the account email in Settings and sign in with the one you intend to use.

## Cause 4: a loading or sync glitch

If history is on, you are on the right account, and you are not in Temporary Chat, but new chats still do not appear, it is likely a display or sync issue. Universal browser-level fixes resolve most of these:

1. Hard refresh the page.
2. Clear the site's cached files and cookies, then sign back in.
3. Disable extensions or open ChatGPT in an incognito window to test.
4. Try a different browser or device on the same account.
5. Check the official status page for an outage.

If the chats reappear, it was a loading problem, not data loss. For a fuller walkthrough, see [ChatGPT history not loading: how to fix it](/blog/chatgpt-history-not-loading-fix), and if existing chats vanished rather than new ones failing to save, see [ChatGPT conversation disappeared](/blog/chatgpt-conversation-disappeared).

## How to make sure conversations are always kept

The underlying issue is that whether a conversation is saved depends entirely on ChatGPT's settings and your account state. One wrong toggle or a glitch, and the record is gone or hidden.

A local-first archive removes that dependency. LLMnesia is a free, local-first Chrome extension that searches your AI chat history across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and 10+ platforms. It indexes your conversations on your own device as you browse them, so you keep a searchable copy independent of ChatGPT's history setting. Even if a setting changes or a glitch hides your sidebar, your indexed record remains, and you can search it by content. The one exception is Temporary Chat: if a conversation was never displayed as a normal chat, there is nothing to index, so use a normal chat for anything you want kept.

## Quick checklist

1. Are you in a Temporary Chat? Switch to a normal chat.
2. Is chat history on in Data Controls? Turn it on.
3. Are you on the right account? Confirm the email in Settings.
4. Still not appearing? Hard refresh, clear cache, check status.
5. Keep a local-first record so saving never depends on a single setting.
