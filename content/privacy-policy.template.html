Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 June 2026

Overview

LLMnesia is a browser extension that indexes your AI conversations locally on your device. Your conversation content, search queries, conversation URLs, and titles stay in your browser and are not sent to LLMnesia servers.

We collect limited anonymous analytics to understand product usage, and we collect email addresses only when someone explicitly opts in for updates or contact.

Information the extension collects

Your AI conversation content stays local. The extension stores the conversation index and settings in your browser using IndexedDB and chrome.storage.local.

The extension sends anonymous product analytics through PostHog's EU ingestion endpoint. These analytics include only:

  • Anonymous install ID
  • Event names such as extension_opened, search_performed, and result_opened
  • Extension version
  • Platform source such as chatgpt, claude, or gemini
  • Timestamps and basic numeric counts or status values

PostHog analytics are anonymous only. If you opt in with an email address in the extension, that email is used for the update list and is not sent to PostHog. Extension analytics events include PostHog's GeoIP disable flag so they are not enriched with IP-derived location properties.

Information the website collects

The LLMnesia website uses Google Analytics when a Google Analytics ID is configured. Website analytics may include page views, page paths, content groups, and simple events such as an email signup event. Email addresses are not sent to Google Analytics by LLMnesia.

If you submit an email address through the website email signup form, the email is sent to a Google Apps Script endpoint and stored in a Google Sheet used for product updates.

If you use the contact form, your submitted name, email address, message, and selected contact type are sent through Web3Forms so we can receive and respond to your message.

What we do not collect

  • No chat content or message text is sent to LLMnesia analytics
  • No search query text is sent to LLMnesia analytics
  • No conversation URLs or titles are sent to LLMnesia analytics
  • No email addresses are sent to PostHog analytics
  • No browser fingerprinting or advertising tracking is used by the extension
  • No LLMnesia account is required

How your extension data is stored

All data created by the extension, including your conversation index and extension settings, is stored locally in your browser using IndexedDB and Chrome extension storage. This data remains on your device unless you export it yourself.

You can view, export, or delete all stored extension data at any time through the extension's settings.

Browser permissions

LLMnesia requests the following browser permissions, each used solely to provide core functionality:

  • Storage — saves your conversation index and extension settings locally in your browser.
  • Tabs — opens the correct conversation when you click a search result.
  • Active Tab — detects which supported AI platform you are currently visiting.
  • Scripting — injects the search overlay and indexing scripts into supported AI chat pages.

Host permissions

LLMnesia requests host permissions for supported AI platforms only:

  • ChatGPT (chatgpt.com, chat.openai.com)
  • Claude (claude.ai)
  • Gemini (gemini.google.com)
  • DeepSeek (deepseek.com, chat.deepseek.com)
  • Perplexity (perplexity.ai)
  • Grok (grok.com)
  • Mistral (chat.mistral.ai, mistral.ai)
  • Kimi (kimi.com)
  • Qwen (qwen.ai, chat.qwen.ai)
  • Google AI Studio (aistudio.google.com)

These permissions are used to index conversation content locally on your device and to open conversations when you select search results.

Third-party services

Service Purpose Data sent
PostHog (EU) Extension product analytics Anonymous install ID, event names, extension version, platform source, timestamps, and basic counts/statuses
Google Analytics Website analytics Page views, page paths, content groups, and simple website events
Google Apps Script / Google Sheets Email signup and update list management Email addresses submitted through extension or website opt-in forms
Web3Forms Website contact form delivery Name, email address, contact type, and message submitted through the contact form

Data sharing

We do not sell user data. We use the third-party services listed above only to operate analytics, email signup, and contact/update workflows.

Data retention and deletion

Extension data is stored locally on your device. You can delete it by:

  • Using the export/delete controls within the extension's settings
  • Uninstalling the extension
  • Clearing your browser's extension storage

To request deletion of analytics or email signup records held by third-party services, contact us through the website contact form.

Children's privacy

LLMnesia is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we update this privacy policy, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the extension or website after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please reach out via the contact form on our website.
