07 — privacy

Local-first. Private by design.

LLMnesia stores your indexed conversations on your device, not on our servers.

Stored locally

Your index lives in your browser's IndexedDB, on this device only.

No servers

Nothing is uploaded, synced, or sent to the cloud. Ever.

You're in control

Export or wipe everything with one click. No account needed.

  • No account required
  • No conversation uploads
  • 0 bytes leave your device

Designed for people who value privacy.

What each browser permission does
  • Storage - saves your index and settings locally in your browser
  • Tabs - opens the right conversation when you click a search result
  • Active tab - detects which AI platform you're currently visiting
  • Scripting - adds the search bar and index indicator to AI chat pages

No permission is used to read data from any site other than the supported AI platforms.

12 — answers

Frequently asked questions

Is my data stored on your servers?

No. Everything is stored locally in your browser. LLMnesia never sends your conversations, search queries, or personal data to external servers.

How does local indexing actually work?

When you visit a supported AI platform, LLMnesia indexes conversation content locally in your browser storage so it is searchable later. It runs automatically in the background.

Will it index my older chat history too?

Yes. You can import your existing history from {{IMPORT_PLATFORM_LIST}} in a few clicks. For other supported platforms, LLMnesia indexes conversations automatically as you open them.

Which AI platforms are supported today?

Supported now: {{PLATFORM_LIST}}. You can also index your local Claude Code sessions — from the terminal, the VS Code extension, and the desktop app — into the same search. Codex session support is coming soon.

Will it slow down my browser?

LLMnesia is designed to stay lightweight. Indexing happens incrementally in the background while you work.

Can I delete my indexed data?

Yes. You can clear all indexed data with one click from the extension settings.

Can I export my conversations?

Yes. You can export and back up your conversation history in a portable format that you own.

Does it require an account?

No account is required for local indexing and search.

Is it free?

Yes. LLMnesia is completely free to use.
