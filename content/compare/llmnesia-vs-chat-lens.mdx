Most AI chat history comparisons pit a search tool against a save-only one. Chat Lens and LLMnesia are different: both are local-first, both are search-first, and both exist to help you recover answers from past AI sessions. This is the most direct, apples-to-apples comparison in the category, so the decision comes down to feature breadth versus focus.

## LLMnesia vs Chat Lens at a glance

| | Chat Lens | LLMnesia |
|---|---|---|
| Platform coverage | 18+ platforms | Major platforms, 10+ total |
| Search | Instant keyword search | Full-text, search-first |
| Indexing | Local archive | Automatic background indexing |
| Jump-back to original chat | From results | Direct link |
| Extra features | Prompt library, media gallery, duplicate detection (beta) | Focused retrieval, no sprawl |
| Storage | 100% local | Local-first |
| Price | In-app purchases | Free |

## What Chat Lens does

Chat Lens (4.8 stars) supports search across 18+ AI platforms, with a feature set that goes beyond pure retrieval:

- **Instant search:** find any conversation by keyword, question, or topic.
- **Prompt library:** save and reuse prompts with smart suggestions.
- **Bookmark with keywords:** tag important chats for quick retrieval.
- **Media and files gallery:** browse AI-generated images and files in one place.
- **Smart suggestions (beta):** detects duplicate questions, surfaces relevant past conversations, and suggests starting fresh when a topic drifts.
- **Keyboard shortcuts:** Tab to open, arrow keys to browse, Enter to select.
- **100% local archive:** no servers or cloud sync.
- In-app purchases for additional features.

## What LLMnesia does

- **Automatic background indexing:** sessions are indexed as you work, with no manual step.
- **Full-text search** across supported platforms with direct jump-back links.
- **Local-first:** your index stays on your device.
- A clean, focused retrieval interface with no extra modules to manage.

## The smart-suggestion angle

Chat Lens's duplicate detection is worth calling out: it proactively flags when you are about to re-ask something you already solved, nudging you at prompt time instead of waiting for a search. That is a genuinely different approach to the same underlying problem. Whether it feels helpful or intrusive depends on how you work; LLMnesia takes the quieter path of indexing everything and staying out of the way until you search.

## A concrete example

You are about to ask Claude how to structure a research interview, a question you worked through weeks ago in ChatGPT. Chat Lens might surface that earlier session as a duplicate before you finish typing. With LLMnesia, you search `interview guide structure` when you realise you have done this before, and jump straight back to the original ChatGPT conversation. Both routes recover the answer; one is proactive, the other is on-demand. Either way, the goal is the retrieval covered in [find old ChatGPT conversations](/blog/find-old-chatgpt-conversations).

## Can you use both together?

You can, but because they overlap so closely, most people pick one. Choose Chat Lens if you want the bundled prompt library, media gallery, and proactive suggestions in the same extension. Choose LLMnesia if you want a lean, free, search-first tool that indexes automatically and gets out of the way.

## Choosing between them

**Chat Lens is a better fit if:**
- You want maximum platform coverage (18+).
- The duplicate detection and proactive suggestions suit how you work.
- You want a prompt library and media gallery bundled in.

**LLMnesia is a better fit if:**
- You prefer a focused, search-first tool with no extra modules.
- Automatic background indexing with minimal configuration matters.
- You want a streamlined jump-back-to-original experience, free.
