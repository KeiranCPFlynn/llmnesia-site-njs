AI Toolbox and LLMnesia both help you recover work from past AI conversations. Both offer cross-platform search. AI Toolbox adds an extensive set of tools inside AI websites, while LLMnesia combines a local history archive with free search and a connection that lets desktop assistants use that archive.

This comparison is published by LLMnesia. Competitor details were checked against the linked vendor documentation on 18 October 2026, rather than a hands-on benchmark.

## The practical differences

| Need | AI Toolbox | LLMnesia |
|---|---|---|
| Search across AI websites | ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Grok | Supported web platforms plus local coding sessions |
| Free search | Five newest results per query | Unmetered local keyword and semantic search |
| Organise work in the AI sidebar | Folders, bookmarks, saved prompts and prompt chains | Search, saved memories and source links |
| Download a readable chat | Single-chat TXT free; other formats on Premium | Portable history backup; use a dedicated exporter for formatted documents |
| Reuse context in another chat | In-chat context mentions; long threads may be summarised | Free local MCP retrieval for compatible desktop assistants |
| Cross-device options | Organisational metadata sync | Optional paid Vault with end-to-end encrypted backup and sync |

## When AI Toolbox fits better

AI Toolbox is worth considering if most of your work happens inside its four supported AI websites and you want folders, reusable prompts, bulk operations and downloadable conversations in the same interface. Its free tier includes limited folders, prompts and bookmarks, alongside unlimited single-chat TXT exports. PDF, Markdown and JSON export require Premium.

You do not need to file every conversation before searching. Its [search documentation](https://www.ai-toolbox.co/tutorials/all-platforms/search-all-platforms) describes an on-device index, enabled separately for cross-platform features. Open each supported site with the relevant module switched on to make its chats available. Free search returns the five newest matches; Premium exposes all results and additional filters.

Its context mentions can also bring a selected past chat into a new prompt, with automatic summarisation of long threads.

That makes the decision concrete: if you need a polished TXT copy of one conversation today, the free exporter may already do the job. If you want a broader sidebar toolkit, evaluate its paid features against your actual workflow.

## When LLMnesia fits better

LLMnesia suits people who regularly need to find a past answer across several AI tools, or want a desktop assistant to consult their previous work. Import supported older history, let capture keep the archive current, and search by keyword or meaning. Local Claude Code, Codex and ZCode sessions can sit alongside your captured web chats.

The [free MCP connection](/mcp) adds a different way to retrieve context. Ask a compatible desktop assistant, "What did we decide about the onboarding flow?" It can search the saved history, open relevant conversations and use their contents in its answer, with links back to the sources. The extension must have captured or imported those conversations first.

This does not restore ChatGPT threads inside Claude's sidebar. It makes saved conversations available as reference material while you work in another assistant. See the [AI-history portability guide](/blog/how-to-move-ai-chat-history-between-assistants) for file-based alternatives.

## Price and privacy

AI Toolbox's pricing page lists Premium at $9.99 monthly per platform, $99 lifetime per platform, or $199 lifetime for All Access. Verify the selected module and billing terms before purchasing. The free TXT export limit is not three downloads daily; its current site says unlimited single-chat TXT export.

AI Toolbox says conversation content stays in the browser, while folders, prompts and tags can sync through its servers, encrypted at rest. Keeping the search index local does not mean every account or sync feature is offline.

LLMnesia's local capture, imports, search and desktop MCP connection are free and require no account. Optional [Vault](/vault) adds paid, end-to-end encrypted sync and backup. Limited product analytics exclude conversation text and search queries. When a connected assistant reads an old chat, the retrieved text is handled under that assistant's privacy terms.

Choose based on the work you want to do next: organising and exporting within the supported AI sites, finding material across your archive, or letting a desktop assistant retrieve it for you.
