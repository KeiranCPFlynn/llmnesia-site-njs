Perplexity automatically saves your threads in a Library and lets you group them into Spaces, all inside Perplexity. LLMnesia is a free, local-first Chrome extension that indexes the full text of your conversations across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity and 10+ platforms from one search. If you research in several tools, LLMnesia gives you one place to find everything; if you stay entirely in Perplexity, its native Library may cover you.

## LLMnesia vs Perplexity Library at a glance

| | Perplexity Library | LLMnesia |
|---|---|---|
| Platforms covered | Perplexity only | Perplexity, ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and 10+ more |
| Search scope | Your Perplexity threads | Full text across all platforms |
| Organisation | Spaces and Collections | Search-first, no manual filing |
| Jump-back to original chat | Inside Perplexity | Direct link, any platform |

| Price | Included; some Pro features | Free |
| Best for | Perplexity-only research | Multi-tool retrieval |

## What Perplexity Library does

Perplexity is an AI answer engine that returns sourced, cited responses. Its Library automatically saves your threads, the searches and the AI's answers, so you can revisit them without re-running the query. You can search the Library by keyword or title, and organise threads into Spaces: topic or project hubs that combine your threads, uploaded files, and tailored instructions. For someone who runs their research primarily in Perplexity, this is a useful in-product memory.

## The single-platform ceiling

The Library's value is entirely within Perplexity. That is fine if your research lives exclusively there, but most knowledge workers move between tools:

- Research in Perplexity
- Reasoning and synthesis in Claude
- Code and implementation in ChatGPT
- Drafting in Claude or ChatGPT

The moment you leave Perplexity, the Library cannot help you find what you did next. A decision made two tools ago is simply not in it.

## What LLMnesia does differently

LLMnesia indexes conversations across all supported AI platforms into one searchable index, so you search once and get results from wherever the answer actually was.

- **Cross-platform:** one search covers all your AI tools, not just Perplexity.
- **Automatic indexing:** conversations are captured as you have them, with no manual save.
- **Jump-back links:** results link to the original conversation on the original platform.
- **Local-first:** your index stays on your device, not in a cloud account.

## A concrete example: research to writing

A typical workflow where the Library falls short:

1. You research a topic in Perplexity, and the findings sit in your Library.
2. You synthesise in Claude, which the Library never sees.
3. You draft in ChatGPT, also outside the Library.
4. Three weeks later you need the original cited source from step 1 and the synthesis from step 2.

With Perplexity Library you can find step 1, but step 2 means scrolling Claude history by hand. With LLMnesia you search `topic research [keyword]` and get results from both Perplexity and Claude in one view. This stitched-together flow is the subject of the [cross-LLM workflow guide](/blog/cross-llm-workflow-without-context-loss).

## Can you use both together?

Yes. Keep using Perplexity Spaces to organise active research inside Perplexity, and let LLMnesia provide the cross-tool search that connects that research to whatever you did in Claude, ChatGPT, or Gemini afterward.

## Choosing between them

**Perplexity Library is the right choice if:**
- Your research workflow is exclusively within Perplexity.
- You mainly need to revisit Perplexity threads and their cited sources.
- You rarely move between Perplexity and other AI tools.

**LLMnesia is the better fit if:**
- You use Perplexity alongside ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini.
- You need to find information from any AI session, not just Perplexity threads.
- You want one search interface for your entire AI conversation history.
