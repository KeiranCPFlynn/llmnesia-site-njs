Superpower ChatGPT and LLMnesia treat the same symptom at different scopes. Superpower ChatGPT enhances the ChatGPT interface with folders, prompts, and organisation tools. LLMnesia is a free, local-first Chrome extension that searches the full text of your conversations across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and 10+ platforms. If you live in ChatGPT alone, Superpower may be enough; if you use several tools, LLMnesia is the retrieval layer that ties them together.

## LLMnesia vs Superpower ChatGPT at a glance

| | Superpower ChatGPT | LLMnesia |
|---|---|---|
| Platforms covered | ChatGPT only | ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and 10+ more |
| Core focus | In-product UI enhancements | Cross-platform retrieval |
| Search depth | Search and highlight (chat-level) | Full text of every message |
| Folders, prompt chains, model switcher | Yes | No |
| Jump-back across platforms | ChatGPT only | Any platform, direct link |
| Where your data lives | Your OpenAI account | Local-first, on your device |
| Price | Free with paid Pro tier | Free |

## What Superpower ChatGPT does well

Superpower packs a large feature set into one extension:

- **Folders and subfolders:** organise ChatGPT conversations with colour-coded, drag-and-drop folders.
- **Search and highlight:** search across previous ChatGPT chats with highlighted results.
- **Export:** export chats to PDF, .txt, .json, or .md.
- **Prompt chains:** save and run a series of prompts with one click.
- **Model switcher:** change the GPT model mid-conversation.
- **Prompt Optimizer and Notes (Pro):** AI-assisted prompt cleanup and per-chat notes synced across devices.
- **Auto-delete and auto-archive:** manage old conversations automatically.

These are well-executed features for heavy ChatGPT users, and the extension is actively maintained and widely used.

## The hard limit: ChatGPT only

Every Superpower feature stops at the chatgpt.com boundary. If you run a question through Claude and get a useful answer, Superpower cannot find it. If you use Gemini for research, that history is invisible to its search. As more teams adopt several AI tools, this single-platform ceiling becomes a daily friction point. Reviews also note that Superpower's search leans toward chat-level matching rather than deep full-text recall, which matters once you need a specific phrase from inside a long thread.

## Where LLMnesia is different

- **Multi-platform coverage:** one search index spanning ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and more.
- **Full-text search:** match a phrase, error message, or code snippet inside any conversation.
- **Background indexing:** automatic, with no in-product configuration.
- **Jump-back links:** results link straight to the original conversation on whichever platform it came from.
- **Local-first:** your index stays on your device.

## A concrete example

You solved a tricky regex in ChatGPT, then a week later refined it further in Claude. Today you need the final version. Superpower can search your ChatGPT chats but is blind to the Claude follow-up, and its title-leaning search may not surface the exact message. With LLMnesia you search `regex lookbehind email` and see both the ChatGPT origin and the Claude refinement in one list, each with a jump-back link. ## Can you use both together?

Yes, and for many heavy ChatGPT users that is the ideal setup. Keep Superpower for strong in-product organisation inside ChatGPT (folders, prompt chains, exports), and add LLMnesia for full-text retrieval that also reaches your Claude, Gemini, and other sessions. They do not overlap or conflict.

## Choosing between them

**Use Superpower ChatGPT if:**
- Your AI workflow is exclusively inside ChatGPT.
- You want deep UI enhancements: folders, model switcher, prompt chains, notes.
- You want features beyond retrieval, like exports and archiving.

**Use LLMnesia if:**
- You use ChatGPT alongside Claude, Gemini, or other AI tools.
- Your retrieval problem spans platforms, not just one product.
- You want full-text search that works regardless of which AI tool you used.
