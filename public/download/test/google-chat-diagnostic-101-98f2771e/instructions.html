Google chat diagnostic

A temporary check for your affected Google AI Mode conversation. Keep your normal LLMnesia extension installed.

Download diagnostic ZIP (12 KB)

Google chat diagnostic, version 1.0.1

This temporary tool checks only the Google AI Mode conversation you open.
It does not change your saved LLMnesia chats or send anything automatically.
The report contains message counts and technical results, not message text,
titles, account details, chat IDs, Google URLs or access tokens.

SET UP ONCE
1. Unzip the downloaded file. Keep the extracted folder until you finish.
2. In Chrome, enter chrome://extensions in the address bar.
3. Turn on Developer mode at the top right.
4. Click Load unpacked. Choose the extracted folder containing manifest.json.
   If there is another folder inside, choose that inner folder.
   Leave your existing LLMnesia extension installed.

RUN THE CHECK
5. Open the affected conversation from Google's AI Mode history and let the
   visible answer finish loading. Save any unsent draft somewhere first.
   Do not type or switch chats during the check.
6. Click Chrome's puzzle-piece Extensions button, then
   "LLMnesia Google chat diagnostic". Click Run check.
7. The Google chat reloads once. A small check panel appears on that page.
   Keep the chat open for up to 40 seconds.
   The report downloads automatically. If the download does not appear,
   click Save report again. You can preview its contents in the check panel.
8. Reply to Keiran's email and attach llmnesia-google-chat-check-1.0.1.json.
   You do not need to send your conversation or your Google export.

AFTERWARD
9. At chrome://extensions, remove ONLY "LLMnesia Google chat diagnostic".
   Keep the normal LLMnesia extension. You can turn Developer mode off again.

If Chrome says installation is blocked by your organisation, or Load unpacked
is unavailable, stop and tell Keiran. Do not change security or account policy.

This check may identify messages Google's page omitted or explain why a source
response could not be inspected. It cannot prove that Google has deleted older
messages, and it does not recover or upload a transcript.

The report does not include your message text. Reply to Keiran with the downloaded JSON report. You do not need to send your conversation.
